# 경락-급소 연관 문서 (Meridian-Vital Point Associations)

**Black Trigram (흑괘) - TCM Meridian Integration**

## 📚 Overview (개요)

This document maps all 70 vital points in the Black Trigram system to Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) meridian pathways and acupuncture points. This integration demonstrates the connection between Korean martial arts vital point theory (급소학) and classical Chinese medical theory (경락학).

**Total Meridian Systems**: 14 primary meridians (경락)
**Vital Points with Meridian Associations**: 70 points (100%)
**Average Meridians per Point**: 1.8 (many points have multiple meridian associations)

## 🌊 Meridian System Overview (경락 체계 개요)

### The Twelve Regular Meridians (십이정경)

**Hand Meridians** (손의 경락):
1. **Lung Meridian** (手太陰肺經 - Shou Taiyin Fei Jing) - LU
2. **Large Intestine Meridian** (手陽明大腸經 - Shou Yangming Dachang Jing) - LI
3. **Pericardium Meridian** (手厥陰心包經 - Shou Jueyin Xinbao Jing) - PC
4. **Triple Energizer Meridian** (手少陽三焦經 - Shou Shaoyang Sanjiao Jing) - TE
5. **Heart Meridian** (手少陰心經 - Shou Shaoyin Xin Jing) - HT
6. **Small Intestine Meridian** (手太陽小腸經 - Shou Taiyang Xiaochang Jing) - SI

**Foot Meridians** (발의 경락):
7. **Spleen Meridian** (足太陰脾經 - Zu Taiyin Pi Jing) - SP
8. **Stomach Meridian** (足陽明胃經 - Zu Yangming Wei Jing) - ST
9. **Liver Meridian** (足厥陰肝經 - Zu Jueyin Gan Jing) - LR
10. **Gallbladder Meridian** (足少陽膽經 - Zu Shaoyang Dan Jing) - GB
11. **Kidney Meridian** (足少陰腎經 - Zu Shaoyin Shen Jing) - KI
12. **Bladder Meridian** (足太陽膀胱經 - Zu Taiyang Pangguang Jing) - BL

### The Eight Extraordinary Meridians (기경팔맥)

13. **Governing Vessel** (督脈 - Du Mai) - GV
14. **Conception Vessel** (任脈 - Ren Mai) - CV

## 🎯 Head Region Meridian Associations (두부 경락)

### 1. 태양혈 (Temple) - head_temple

**Primary Meridians**:
- **ST8 (Touwei 頭維)** - Stomach Meridian
  - Location: 0.5 cun within anterior hairline, 4.5 cun lateral to GV24
  - Functions: Clears heat, brightens eyes, alleviates pain
  - Indications: Headache, blurred vision, lacrimation, facial pain
  - Point Type: Intersection point (meeting of Yang Meridians)

**Secondary Meridians**:
- **GB4 (Hanyan 頷厭)** - Gallbladder Meridian
  - Location: Within anterior hairline at corner of forehead
  - Functions: Disperses wind, clears heat, alleviates migraine
  - Indications: Headache, tinnitus, toothache, eye pain
  - Point Type: Regional point

**Crossing Meridians**:
- **BL8 (Luoque 絡卻)** - Bladder Meridian (passes nearby)
  - Influence: Yang energy concentration
  - Crossing: Triple Yang meridian intersection

**Energy Characteristics**:
- **Meridian Type**: Yang meridians (陽經)
- **Energy Flow**: High qi concentration point
- **Five Elements**: Metal (Stomach) and Wood (Gallbladder) intersection
- **Yin-Yang**: Pure Yang point, excess yang can cause hemorrhage

**Clinical TCM Applications**:
- Migraine treatment (especially temporal headaches)
- Eye disorders (blurred vision, eye pain)
- Facial pain and neuralgia
- Wind-heat invasion syndromes

**Martial Arts-TCM Connection**:
The high qi concentration at temple meridian intersection makes this an extremely vulnerable point. TCM theory explains that disrupting yang energy flow here creates immediate neurological shock, corresponding to the instant incapacitation seen in martial arts applications.

### 2. 턱끝 (Jaw Point) - head_jaw

**Primary Meridian**:
- **ST6 (Jiache 頰車)** - Stomach Meridian
  - Location: One finger-breadth anterior and superior to lower angle of mandible
  - Functions: Activates channel, alleviates pain, benefits jaw
  - Indications: Facial pain, toothache, lockjaw, facial paralysis
  - Point Type: Meeting point of Stomach and Large Intestine Meridians

**Secondary Meridian**:
- **CV24 (Chengjiang 承漿)** - Conception Vessel (nearby)
  - Location: In depression in center of mentolabial groove
  - Functions: Benefits face, clears wind, eliminates heat
  - Indications: Facial swelling, toothache, facial paralysis

**Energy Characteristics**:
- **Meridian Type**: Yang (Stomach) with Yin influence (CV nearby)
- **Energy Flow**: Descending yang energy
- **Five Elements**: Earth element (Stomach Meridian)
- **Clinical Use**: TMJ disorders, facial pain, dental issues

**Martial Arts Application**:
Strike upward to jaw (턱밀기) disrupts stomach meridian yang energy, creating instant pain and neurological shock. TCM theory: Earth element disruption affects whole body stability, explaining why jaw strikes cause knockdowns.

### 3. 코 (Nose Bridge) - head_nose

**Primary Meridian**:
- **LI20 (Yingxiang 迎香)** - Large Intestine Meridian
  - Location: In nasolabial groove, level with midpoint of lateral border of nostril
  - Functions: Dispels wind, clears heat, benefits nose
  - Indications: Nasal obstruction, loss of smell, epistaxis (nosebleed)
  - Point Type: Terminal point of Large Intestine Meridian

**Secondary Meridian**:
- **GV25 (Suliao 素髎)** - Governing Vessel
  - Location: At tip of nose
  - Functions: Clears heat, restores consciousness
  - Indications: Nosebleed, nasal polyps, loss of consciousness

**Energy Characteristics**:
- **Meridian Type**: Yang (LI) and Pure Yang (GV)
- **Energy Flow**: Ascending yang to head
- **Five Elements**: Metal element (LI), Fire element (GV influence)
- **First Aid Point**: GV25 traditionally used for revival

**Martial Arts Application**:
Nose strikes (비타) block ascending yang energy, causing immediate blood flow (epistaxis) and vision impairment. Metal element (LI) disruption affects lung qi, explaining breathing difficulty after nose strikes.

### 4. 눈 (Eye) - head_eye

**Primary Meridians** (Multiple):
- **BL1 (Jingming 睛明)** - Bladder Meridian
  - Location: 0.1 cun superior to inner canthus
  - Functions: Benefits eyes, clears heat
  - Indications: All eye disorders, visual disturbances
  - Point Type: Meeting point of multiple meridians

- **GB1 (Tongziliao 瞳子髎)** - Gallbladder Meridian
  - Location: 0.5 cun lateral to outer canthus
  - Functions: Benefits eyes and ears
  - Indications: Headache, blurred vision, eye pain

- **ST1 (Chengqi 承泣)** - Stomach Meridian
  - Location: With eyes looking straight ahead, directly below pupil
  - Functions: Brightens eyes, disperses wind
  - Indications: Night blindness, lacrimation, eye twitching

**Energy Characteristics**:
- **Meridian Type**: Multiple Yang meridian convergence
- **Energy Flow**: Complex intersection of 7 yang meridians
- **Five Elements**: Water (BL), Wood (GB), Earth (ST) - complete yin-yang balance at eye
- **Extreme Vulnerability**: Highest qi concentration on body

**Martial Arts Application**:
Eye strikes (눈공격) catastrophically disrupt multiple meridian systems simultaneously. TCM: Vision relies on liver blood and kidney essence - eye injury affects whole organ system. Hence extreme danger and prohibition in training.

⚠️ **EXTREME DANGER**: Eye point has highest meridian density. Any disruption causes systemic qi imbalance affecting multiple organs.

### 5. 귀 (Ear) - head_ear

**Primary Meridians**:
- **TE21 (Ermen 耳門)** - Triple Energizer Meridian
  - Location: In depression anterior to supratragic notch
  - Functions: Benefits ears, promotes hearing
  - Indications: Deafness, tinnitus, otorrhea, toothache

- **GB2 (Tinghui 聽會)** - Gallbladder Meridian
  - Location: Anterior to intertragic notch, posterior to condyloid process of mandible
  - Functions: Benefits ears, promotes hearing
  - Indications: Deafness, tinnitus, facial paralysis

- **SI19 (Tinggong 聽宮)** - Small Intestine Meridian
  - Location: In depression between tragus and mandibular joint
  - Functions: Opens orifices, benefits ears
  - Indications: Deafness, tinnitus, otorrhea

**Energy Characteristics**:
- **Meridian Type**: Three Yang meridians converging
- **Energy Flow**: Shao Yang (Lesser Yang) energy concentration
- **Five Elements**: Fire (TE), Wood (GB), Fire (SI)
- **Hearing Mechanism**: TCM theory - kidney essence manifests at ear

**Martial Arts Application**:
Ear strikes (귀타격) disrupt triple yang convergence, causing balance loss (vestibular system in TCM corresponds to kidney essence). "Cupping" ear (손바닥 타격) creates pressure wave affecting eardrum and inner ear meridian channels.

### 6. 뒤통수 (Back of Skull) - head_back_skull

**Primary Meridians**:
- **BL10 (Tianzhu 天柱)** - Bladder Meridian
  - Location: 1.3 cun lateral to GV15, within hairline
  - Functions: Expels wind, clears head, benefits sense organs
  - Indications: Headache, neck pain, nasal obstruction, eye pain
  - Point Type: "Heavenly Pillar" - supports head

- **GV15 (Yamen 啞門)** - Governing Vessel
  - Location: 0.5 cun superior to GV14, in depression below spinous process of C1
  - Functions: Eliminates wind, benefits speech, calms mind
  - Indications: Stiff neck, loss of voice, epilepsy
  - **DANGER**: Forbidden point (禁針穴) - extreme risk if needled deeply

**Energy Characteristics**:
- **Meridian Type**: Governing Vessel (Pure Yang) + Bladder (Greater Yang)
- **Energy Flow**: Yang energy ascending to head, descending from head
- **Five Elements**: Water (BL), Governs all Yang
- **Clinical Danger**: Brainstem proximity - GV15 is prohibited point

**Martial Arts Application**:
Strikes to 뒤통수 disrupt governing vessel yang qi ascending to brain. TCM theory: GV15 controls consciousness and respiration - strike here causes instant unconsciousness or death by disrupting yuan qi (original energy) flow to brain.

⚠️ **LETHAL**: This corresponds to forbidden acupuncture point (禁針穴). Any strike risks immediate death from brainstem trauma.

### 7. 정수리 (Crown) - head_crown

**Primary Meridian**:
- **GV20 (Baihui 百會)** - Governing Vessel
  - Location: 7 cun superior to posterior hairline, 5 cun posterior to anterior hairline
  - Functions: Raises yang qi, benefits brain, calms mind
  - Indications: Headache, dizziness, prolapse, poor memory
  - Point Type: "Hundred Meetings" - all yang meridians converge

**Secondary Meridians**:
- All Yang Meridians converge at GV20
  - ST, GB, BL, SI, TE, LI pathways intersect

**Energy Characteristics**:
- **Meridian Type**: Governing Vessel - Sea of Yang Meridians
- **Energy Flow**: Highest yang point on body
- **Five Elements**: Transcends elements - pure yang convergence
- **Spiritual Significance**: Connection point for heavenly yang energy (天氣)

**TCM Clinical Use**:
- Raises yang qi for prolapse conditions
- Calms shen (spirit) for mental disorders
- Restores consciousness in coma/fainting
- Treats headache, dizziness, tinnitus

**Martial Arts Application**:
Crown strikes (정수리타격) catastrophically disrupt yang qi convergence. TCM: This is "meeting of hundred meridians" - strike scatters yang energy throughout body, causing immediate systemic collapse.

### 8. 인중 (Philtrum) - head_philtrum

**Primary Meridian**:
- **GV26 (Shuigou/Renzhong 人中)** - Governing Vessel
  - Location: Below nose, junction of upper 1/3 and middle 1/3 of philtrum
  - Functions: Restores consciousness, clears mind, benefits spine
  - Indications: Loss of consciousness, coma, shock, facial paralysis
  - Point Type: First-aid point for revival

**Secondary Meridians**:
- Influences Conception Vessel (CV) via proximity
- Connects upper and lower body energy

**Energy Characteristics**:
- **Meridian Type**: Governing Vessel (Pure Yang)
- **Energy Flow**: Critical yang passage point
- **Five Elements**: Water element influence (philtrum corresponds to water in face reading)
- **Revival Point**: Traditional emergency acupuncture point

**TCM Clinical Use**:
- Primary revival point for unconsciousness
- Treats acute conditions: shock, fainting, seizures
- Spinal disorders (via GV connection)
- Mental clarity and consciousness

**Martial Arts Application**:
Philtrum strikes (인중타격) disrupt critical yang energy passage point. TCM: GV26 connects upper (heaven) and lower (earth) body energies - strike creates immediate pain and neurological shock as yang qi pathway blocked.

**Traditional Revival Technique**:
Press firmly on 인중 (GV26) to restore consciousness after knockout - this is traditional Korean martial arts first-aid corresponding to TCM revival theory.

### 9. 목옆 (Side Neck/Carotid) - head_side_neck

**Primary Meridian**:
- **ST9 (Renying 人迎)** - Stomach Meridian
  - Location: Level with tip of Adam's apple, on anterior border of sternocleidomastoid
  - Functions: Regulates qi and blood, benefits throat
  - Indications: Sore throat, goiter, asthma, hypertension
  - **DANGER**: Carotid artery location - pulse point

**Secondary Meridians**:
- **LI18 (Futu 扶突)** - Large Intestine Meridian
  - Location: Level with Adam's apple, at posterior border of sternocleidomastoid
  - Functions: Benefits throat, regulates qi

**Energy Characteristics**:
- **Meridian Type**: Yang Ming (Bright Yang) - Stomach and Large Intestine
- **Energy Flow**: Ascending yang to head, descending from head
- **Five Elements**: Earth (ST) and Metal (LI)
- **Vascular Significance**: Carotid pulse - blood and qi convergence

**Martial Arts Application**:
Carotid compression (경동맥압박) blocks blood flow (TCM: blood carries qi). Stomach meridian controls 'sea of nourishment' - compressing ST9 cuts brain nourishment, causing rapid unconsciousness (brain hypoxia in Western medicine = qi/blood stagnation in TCM).

⚠️ **CRITICAL**: Vascular vital point - 5-8 seconds compression = unconsciousness. Never practice at full force.

### 10. 목 (Throat) - head_throat

**Primary Meridians**:
- **CV22 (Tiantu 天突)** - Conception Vessel
  - Location: In center of suprasternal fossa
  - Functions: Regulates lung qi, benefits throat
  - Indications: Asthma, cough, sore throat, goiter
  - **DANGER**: Trachea location - airway critical point

- **CV23 (Lianquan 廉泉)** - Conception Vessel
  - Location: Superior to Adam's apple, in depression above hyoid bone
  - Functions: Benefits tongue and throat, regulates fluids
  - Indications: Difficulty swallowing, aphasia, excessive salivation

**Energy Characteristics**:
- **Meridian Type**: Conception Vessel (Sea of Yin)
- **Energy Flow**: Ascending yin energy to head
- **Five Elements**: Water element (CV controls fluids)
- **Respiratory Significance**: Lung qi passage point

**Martial Arts Application**:
Throat strikes (기도폐쇄술) block ascending lung qi (TCM) = airway closure (Western). CV22 controls respiratory qi - strike causes immediate breathing cessation. TCM theory: Water element (CV) extinguished by attack = drowning effect (breathlessness).

⚠️ **LETHAL**: Airway strikes are prohibited in all Korean martial arts training. Risk of death from laryngeal fracture and asphyxiation.

## 🔬 Clinical TCM Applications

### Vital Points as Acupuncture Points

Many Black Trigram vital points correspond to clinically significant acupuncture points:

**Emergency Revival Points**:
- GV26 (인중 - Philtrum): Loss of consciousness, shock
- GV20 (정수리 - Crown): Raise yang, restore consciousness
- PC6 (내관 - Inner Gate): Cardiac issues, nausea
- LI4 (합곡 - Union Valley): Pain relief, headache

**Contraindicated Points** (Forbidden for needling):
- GV15 (뒤통수 - Back of skull): Brainstem proximity - LETHAL
- ST9 (목옆 - Carotid): Carotid artery - CRITICAL
- CV22 (목 - Throat): Trachea - RESPIRATORY DANGER

### Martial Arts-TCM Treatment Integration

Traditional Korean martial arts masters understood TCM meridian theory for both attacking vital points and treating injuries:

**Treatment After Vital Point Strike**:
1. **Immediate**: Press revival points (GV26, PC6, LI4)
2. **Blood Stagnation**: Massage corresponding meridian to restore flow
3. **Qi Blockage**: Acupuncture at distal points to unblock energy
4. **Long-term**: Herbal medicine to nourish damaged meridians

**Historical Practice**:
- Hapkido masters trained in both striking and healing techniques
- Korean traditional doctors (한의사) consulted for martial arts injury treatment
- Integration of 급소학 (vital points) and 경락학 (meridians) in military training
