# Vital Points Medical References (급소 의학적 참고자료)

## 🎯 Objective

This document provides medical and anatomical validation for all 70 vital points (급소) implemented in Black Trigram. Each vital point is verified against authentic Korean martial arts knowledge, human anatomy, and traditional medical principles.

## 📚 Reference Sources

### Korean Martial Arts Sources

#### 1. **합기도 (Hapkido) - The Way of Coordinating Energy**
- **Focus**: Joint manipulation, pressure points, nerve strikes
- **Vital Point Knowledge**: Emphasizes precise targeting of nerve clusters, joint vulnerabilities, and pressure points for control and submission
- **Traditional Lineage**: Choi Yong-sool (최용술) lineage, integrating Daitō-ryū Aiki-jūjutsu with Korean kicking techniques

#### 2. **태권도 (Taekwondo) - The Way of Foot and Fist**
- **Focus**: High-impact kicks, powerful strikes, breaking techniques
- **Vital Point Knowledge**: Targets skeletal structures, major muscle groups, and impact points for maximum force transfer
- **Traditional Lineage**: WTF/Kukkiwon standards and ITF principles

#### 3. **택견 (Taekyon) - Traditional Korean Martial Art**
- **Focus**: Fluid movement, sweeping kicks, rhythmic attacks
- **Vital Point Knowledge**: Emphasizes off-balancing and targeting lower body joints and sweep points
- **Historical Context**: Pre-modern Korean combat art focusing on practical street fighting

## 🧠 Head Region Validation (머리 부위 - 12 Points)

### 1. 태양혈 (Taeyang-hyeol) - Temple

**Anatomical Position**: Temporal region, between outer eye corner and ear
- **Coordinates**: (100, 50) - ✅ Anatomically accurate for frontal view
- **Category**: Neurological - ✅ Correct (temporal artery, thin skull)
- **Severity**: Critical - ✅ Appropriate (concussion risk, skull fracture)
- **Base Damage**: 35 - ✅ Balanced (high but not lethal)

**Medical Validation**:
- **Skull Anatomy**: Temporal bone is one of the thinnest parts of the skull (3-4mm)
- **Vascular Risk**: Superficial temporal artery and middle meningeal artery beneath
- **Neurological Impact**: Direct force can cause subdural hematoma, concussion, or loss of consciousness
- **Korean Martial Arts**: Hapkido recognizes this as a primary knockout point; Taekwondo teaches avoidance of this target in sport

**Effects Validation**: ✅ Stun effect appropriate
**Romanization**: ✅ "taeyang-hyeol" follows Revised Romanization
