### Principles Implemented

**급소 보호 (Vital Point Protection)**:
- Liver (간), spleen (비장), ribs (늑골) protected by proper guard height

**팔꿈치 보호 (Elbow Protection)**:
- Elbows tight to body (2.0-2.1 rad flexion), not flared

**무릎 추적 (Knee Tracking)**:
- Knees track over toes, no valgus collapse

**무게 중심 (Center of Mass)**:
- Stable pelvis heights, proper weight distribution

**기합 (Kihap - Breath Power)**:
- Exhale on strike, inhale during chamber

**발기술 (Balgisul - Footwork)**:
- Actual stepping with foot lift, not sliding

**견갑골 돌출 (Scapular Protraction)**:
- Additional 7cm reach on strikes
