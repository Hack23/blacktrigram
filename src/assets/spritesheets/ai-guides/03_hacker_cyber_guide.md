# 해커 (Hacker) - Cyber Warrior Spritesheet Generation Guide

## Character Overview

**Archetype**: Cyber Warrior (해커)  
**Philosophy**: Information as power, technological advantage  
**Primary Color**: Cyan (#00FFFF)  
**Secondary Color**: Electric Blue (#0080FF)  
**Combat Focus**: Precise nerve strikes (☲ Li) and Stunning techniques (☳ Jin)  
**Combat Effects**: Temporary paralysis, numbness, disorientation, knockouts

## Visual Design Requirements

### Character Appearance

- **Age**: 20-28 years old, tech-enhanced physique
- **Build**: Slim but defined, optimized for precision and neural targeting
- **Height**: Average height, tech-enhanced (64x128 pixels)
- **Clothing**: High-tech gi with digital circuit patterns, cyberpunk elements
- **Colors**: Cyan and electric blue with subtle holographic effects
- **Hair**: Modern styled, with subtle tech implants visible
- **Facial Features**: Sharp, analytical eyes, possible AR contact lenses
- **Accessories**: AR visor (retractable), tech gauntlets, data interface ports

### Combat Specialization Visual Cues

- **Neural Targeting**: Precision finger sensors, nerve mapping displays
- **Digital Enhancement**: Subtle tech augmentations, enhanced reflexes
- **System Analysis**: Anatomical targeting overlay suggestions
- **Precision Tools**: Digital measurement aids, enhanced accuracy gear
