# 무사 (Musa) - Traditional Korean Warrior Spritesheet Generation Guide

## Character Overview

**Archetype**: Traditional Warrior (무사)  
**Philosophy**: Honor through disciplined strength  
**Primary Color**: Blue (#4A90E2)  
**Secondary Color**: Korean Red (#C8102E)  
**Combat Focus**: Direct force (☰ Geon) and Defensive mastery (☶ Gan)  
**Combat Effects**: Fractures, concussions, immediate trauma, counter-attacks, blocks

## Visual Design Requirements

### Character Appearance

- **Age**: 25-35 years old, battle-hardened veteran
- **Build**: Muscular, disciplined warrior physique with broad shoulders
- **Height**: Tall and imposing (64x128 pixels)
- **Clothing**: Traditional Korean martial arts uniform (도복) with cyberpunk elements
- **Colors**: Deep blue gi with gold trim, traditional Korean patterns
- **Hair**: Short, disciplined military cut, black
- **Facial Features**: Strong jawline, focused expression, Asian features, battle scars
- **Accessories**: Traditional headband (머리띠), black belt with gold detailing

### Combat Specialization Visual Cues

- **Direct Force Focus**: Reinforced knuckles, wrapped hands/wrists for bone impacts
- **Military Background**: Tactical patches, disciplined posture
- **Honor Symbols**: Traditional Korean military insignia
- **Defensive Stance**: Solid, grounded positioning for immovable defense
